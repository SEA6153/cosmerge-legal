Cosmerge — Delete Your Account

This page explains how to permanently delete your Cosmerge account and the data associated with it. Cosmerge is the mobile game published under this developer account; see also our Privacy Policy.

Delete your account inside the app (fastest)

  1. Open Cosmerge
  2. Go to Settings → Account
  3. Tap Delete Account
  4. Confirm with Delete Forever

Deletion takes effect immediately and cannot be undone.

Or request deletion by email

If you can no longer access the app, email blog.sametegeasik@gmail.com from the email address linked to your account with the subject "Delete my Cosmerge account". Requests are processed within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your account and login credentials (email address, password)
  • Your player profile (nickname and tag)
  • Your leaderboard scores and game statistics stored on our servers
  • Your friend connections and pending friend requests

All of the above is removed from our servers immediately when you delete your account. Game progress stored only on your device (settings, local progress) is removed when you uninstall the app.

What may be retained

  • Nothing personally identifiable is retained after account deletion.
  • Anonymous, aggregated technical logs (not linked to your identity) may persist for up to 30 days for service reliability.
  • Advertising data previously processed by Google AdMob is governed by Google's Privacy Policy and can be managed in your device's ad settings.
