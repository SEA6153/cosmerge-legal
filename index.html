Cosmerge — Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Cosmerge ("the App", "we") handles information when you use the App on your device.

Summary

  • Cosmerge does not require an account and does not collect personal information such as your name, email address, or contacts.
  • All game progress (scores, achievements, unlocks, settings) is stored locally on your device and never uploaded to our servers. We operate no servers.
  • The App shows advertisements through Google AdMob, which may process certain device information to serve ads.

Data Stored on Your Device

The App saves the following data locally on your device only:

  • Game progress: scores, high scores, achievements, unlocked content, statistics, daily challenge history.
  • Preferences: sound, music, and haptics settings.

This data never leaves your device. Deleting the App permanently deletes this data. The App's "Reset Progress" option in Settings also erases it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Cosmerge uses Google AdMob to show optional rewarded advertisements (for example, watching an ad to continue a run). When an ad is loaded or shown, Google may collect and process information such as:

  • Device identifiers (such as advertising ID)
  • IP address and coarse location derived from it
  • Device type, operating system, and ad interaction data

We request non-personalized ads only. For details on how Google processes this data, see:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services

Children

Cosmerge does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ads are requested in non-personalized mode.

Purchases

Cosmerge does not currently offer in-app purchases. All in-game currency ("stardust") is earned through gameplay and has no real-world value.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted at this page with an updated date.
